Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CDev (“we”, “us”) collects and uses information when you use chuongle.dev and related marketing tools that connect to social platforms.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information you provide (such as email) when you register for our tools.
  • Connected platform tokens when you authorize a social account (for example TikTok). Tokens are stored to publish content you request and can be revoked by disconnecting the account.
  • Content you upload for scheduling or publishing (images, videos, captions).
  • Technical logs such as approximate timestamps and error logs needed to operate the service.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide scheduling and publishing, keep the service secure, and improve reliability. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing

We share content and necessary account data with the platforms you choose to connect (such as TikTok) only to carry out actions you initiate. We may use infrastructure providers (hosting, databases) under agreements that protect the data.

4. Retention

We keep account and connection data while your account is active, and delete or anonymize it when no longer needed or when you request deletion, except where we must retain records by law.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect credentials and tokens. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Your choices

You can disconnect social accounts at any time, request deletion of your account data by emailing us, and revoke platform access from the platform’s own settings.

7. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the updated date on this page.
